Roof life and replacement age

How long each roof system lasts. With PaxSeal a roof reaches its design life; with reactive repair it wears out sooner by the "life added" setting (capped at half the design life). The model replaces a roof when it reaches this age.

Property types

The leak multiplier scales reactive repair costs for buildings where a leak does more damage. The typical building is what the sales library uses for that type; "replaced at" and "next new roof" show what the model does with it.

Model settings

The settings that apply to every report. The repair multiplier and base repair rate drive most of the savings and have no primary source, so they are the first candidates to calibrate with PAX job data.

Markets

Local replacement costs, cap rates and storm loss for each market. Storm loss comes from FEMA’s expected building loss for the county; override it only with a better local figure.
